Chemistry Syllabus

General Topics

  • Atoms & molecules, Dalton's theory
  • Mole concept, chemical formulae, balanced reactions
  • Stoichiometric calculations (oxidation-reduction, neutralisation, displacement)
  • Concentration: mole fraction, molarity, molality, normality

States of Matter

  • Gas laws, ideal gas equation, absolute temperature scale
  • Deviations from ideality: van der Waals equation
  • Kinetic theory: average, RMS, most probable velocity
  • Law of partial pressures, diffusion
  • Intermolecular forces, vapour pressure, surface tension, viscosity

Atomic Structure

  • Bohr's model, hydrogen spectrum
  • de Broglie hypothesis, wave-particle duality
  • Uncertainty principle, quantum numbers
  • Shapes of s, p, d orbitals
  • Aufbau, Pauli, Hund's rules

Chemical Bonding

  • Covalent bond, orbital overlap
  • Hybridisation: s, p, d
  • Molecular orbital theory (up to Ne₂)
  • Hydrogen bonding, VSEPR model
  • Dipole moment, molecular shapes

Thermodynamics

  • State functions, intensive/extensive properties
  • First law: internal energy, work, heat
  • Enthalpy, heat capacity, Hess's law
  • Second law: entropy, Gibbs energy, spontaneity criteria

Equilibrium

  • Law of mass action, ΔG and ΔG⁰
  • Equilibrium constants (Kp, Kc)
  • Le Chatelier's principle
  • Solubility product, common ion effect
  • Acids & bases (Brønsted, Lewis), buffer, salt hydrolysis

Electrochemistry

  • Electrochemical cells, Nernst equation, EMF
  • Electrode potentials, electrochemical series
  • Faraday’s laws, conductivity (specific, molar, equivalent)
  • Batteries (primary, secondary), fuel cells, corrosion

Chemical Kinetics

  • Rate laws, order, molecularity
  • Half-life, integrated rate laws (0 & 1st order)
  • Arrhenius equation
  • Homogeneous/heterogeneous catalysis, enzymes

Solid State

  • Crystalline solids, 7 crystal systems
  • Packing: fcc, bcc, hcp
  • Point defects, ionic radii, radius ratio

Solutions

  • Henry’s law, Raoult’s law, ideal solutions
  • Colligative properties (VP, BP, FP, OP)
  • van’t Hoff factor

Surface Chemistry

  • Adsorption (Physisorption, Chemisorption)
  • Freundlich isotherm
  • Colloids, emulsions, micelles

Periodic Table

  • Modern periodic law
  • Trends: radius, ionization, electron gain, reactivity, valence

Hydrogen

  • Position, isotopes, hydrides
  • Water, heavy water, hydrogen peroxide
  • Hydrogen as fuel

s-Block Elements

  • Alkali & alkaline earth metals: reactivity, reducing nature
  • Anomalous behaviour of Li, Be
  • Compounds of Na and Ca (uses, properties)

p-Block Elements (Groups 13–18)

  • Oxidation states, trends, anomalies
  • Important compounds of:
  • Group 13: borax, BF₃, AlCl₃
  • Group 14: CO₂, SiO₂, silicones
  • Group 15: NH₃, HNO₃, oxoacids
  • Group 16: SO₂, H₂SO₄
  • Group 17: Cl₂, HCl, oxoacids, interhalogens
  • Group 18: Xenon compounds

d- & f-Block Elements

  • Oxidation states, electrode potentials, alloys
  • Chromium & manganese oxoanions
  • Lanthanoid/actinoid contraction, general characteristics

Coordination Compounds

  • Werner’s theory, nomenclature, isomerism
  • VBT, CFT (octahedral, tetrahedral)
  • Magnetic properties, colours, spectrochemical series
  • Stability, metal carbonyls

Metallurgy

  • Ores & concentration
  • Extraction (Fe, Cu, Zn, Al)
  • Cyanide process (Ag, Au), refining methods

Qualitative Analysis

  • Cations: Ag⁺, Cu²⁺, Fe³⁺, etc.
  • Anions: nitrate, halides (excl. fluoride), carbonate, sulphate, sulphide

Environmental Chemistry

  • Pollution (air, water, soil), industrial waste
  • Green chemistry

Organic Chemistry Basics

  • Hybridisation, aromaticity, isomerism
  • IUPAC naming, hydrogen bonding, inductive/resonance/hyperconjugation
  • Intermediates: carbocations, carbanions, radicals

Hydrocarbons

  • Alkanes: properties, preparation, reactions (halogenation, combustion)
  • Alkenes/Alkynes: electrophilic addition, hydration, KMnO₄ reaction, ozonolysis

Aromatic Compounds

  • Benzene: electrophilic substitution (nitration, halogenation, etc.)
  • Directing effects

Functional Groups

  • Phenols: reactions, synthesis (Kolbe, Reimer-Tiemann)
  • Alkyl halides: Grignard, substitution reactions
  • Alcohols: conversions, reactivity
  • Ethers: Williamson synthesis
  • Aldehydes/Ketones: aldol, Cannizzaro, nucleophilic additions
  • Carboxylic Acids: preparation, reactions
  • Amines: Hoffmann, Gabriel, diazonium salts

Biomolecules

  • Carbohydrates, proteins, nucleic acids
  • Reactions, structure, functions

Polymers

  • Types, examples: nylon, bakelite, PVC
  • Biodegradable polymers

Everyday Chemistry

  • Drugs, detergents, soaps, sweeteners

Organic Practical Chemistry

  • Element detection: N, S, halogens
  • Functional group identification: –OH, –COOH, –NO₂, etc.

Mathematics Syllabus

Sets, Relations, Functions

  • Types of sets, set operations
  • Venn diagrams, De Morgan's laws
  • Relations: ordered pairs, domain, codomain
  • Functions: polynomial, trigonometric, exponential, etc.
  • One-one, onto, inverse, composition

Algebra

  • Complex numbers, conjugates, polar form
  • Fundamental theorem of algebra
  • Quadratic equations: roots, symmetric functions
  • Progressions: AP, GP, sums
  • Logarithms, permutations, combinations
  • Binomial theorem & properties

Matrices & Determinants

  • Types of matrices, operations
  • Determinants (up to 3×3), inverse, adjoint
  • Properties, transformations
  • System of equations (2/3 variables)

Probability & Statistics

  • Probability rules, conditional probability
  • Bayes' theorem, permutations/combinations
  • Central tendency: mean, median, mode
  • Dispersion: variance, SD, grouped/ungrouped data

Trigonometry

  • Trig functions & identities
  • Graphs, multiple angle formulas
  • General solution of trig equations
  • Inverse trig functions & properties

Differential Calculus

  • Limits, continuity, L'Hôpital's Rule
  • Differentiation rules, chain rule
  • Tangents, normals, increasing/decreasing functions
  • Maxima, minima, Rolle's and Lagrange's theorems
  • Implicit functions, higher-order derivatives

Analytical Geometry

  • 2D Geometry
  • Distance, section formula, straight lines
  • Circles: equations, tangents, normals, chords
  • Conics: parabola, ellipse, hyperbola (standard forms, tangents)
  • 3D Geometry
  • Distance, direction cosines/ratios
  • Lines, planes, angles between them
  • Coplanar lines, shortest distance

Integral Calculus

  • Indefinite & definite integrals
  • Fundamental theorem, properties
  • Integration methods: substitution, parts, partial fractions
  • Area under curves
  • Differential equations: formation, solving (separable, linear, homogeneous)

Vectors

  • Addition, scalar multiplication
  • Dot & cross product
  • Scalar & vector triple products, geometry applications

Physics Syllabus

General Topics

  • Units and dimensions, dimensional analysis
  • Least count, significant figures
  • Methods of measurement and error analysis
  • Experiments with various instruments and measurements

Mechanics & Kinematics

  • Motion in one & two dimensions (Cartesian only)
  • Projectiles, uniform circular motion
  • Relative velocity
  • Newton's laws of motion
  • Inertial and non-inertial frames
  • Static & dynamic friction

Work, Energy & Power

  • Kinetic and potential energy
  • Work and power calculations
  • Conservation of momentum and mechanical energy
  • Centre of mass & motion concepts

Rigid Body Dynamics

  • Moment of inertia (M.I.), parallel & perpendicular axis theorems
  • M.I. of simple shapes
  • Angular momentum, torque, conservation of angular momentum
  • Dynamics of rotation, rolling motion

Thermodynamics

  • Thermal expansion (solids, liquids, gases)
  • Calorimetry, latent heat
  • Conduction (1D), convection, radiation
  • Newton's law of cooling
  • Ideal gas laws, specific heats (Cv, Cp)

Electricity and Magnetism

  • Coulomb's law, electric field & potential
  • Energy of point charges & dipoles
  • Field lines, Gauss's law & applications
  • Ohm's law and series & parallel circuits
  • Kirchhoff's laws and applications

Current Electricity (cont.)

  • Heating effect of current

Magnetism (cont.)

  • Biot–Savart’s law, Ampère’s law
  • Magnetic field (straight wire, solenoid, circular coil)
  • Force on charges/wires in a magnetic field
  • Magnetic moment, torque on current loop
  • Moving coil galvanometer, voltmeter, ammeter & conversions

Electromagnetic Induction

  • Faraday’s law, Lenz’s law
  • Self & mutual inductance
  • RC, LR, LC, LCR circuits with AC/DC

Electromagnetic Waves

  • Characteristics of EM waves
  • Electromagnetic spectrum:
  • Radio, microwave, IR, visible, UV, X-ray, gamma
  • Basic uses of each wave

Optics

  • Ray Optics
  • Rectilinear propagation, reflection, refraction
  • Plane and spherical surfaces
  • Total internal reflection, prism dispersion
  • Thin lenses & combinations, magnification
  • Wave Optics
  • Huygens’s principle
  • Young’s double slit experiment (interference)
  • Single slit diffraction
  • Polarization: plane polarized light, Brewster’s law, Polaroids

Modern Physics

  • Atomic nucleus: α, β, γ radiations
  • Radioactive decay laws, half-life, mean life
  • Binding energy, nuclear fission & fusion
  • Photoelectric effect
  • Bohr’s model of hydrogen atom
  • X-rays: characteristic & continuous, Moseley’s law
  • de Broglie waves
